My Dream

From nurse to future teacher

My first choice was becoming a Nurse. I've always wanted to help and care for others — that was my original dream. But life led me down a different path, and I'm now studying to become a Teacher.

I realized teaching is also a beautiful way to serve. Instead of caring for bodies, I'll get to care for hearts and minds. I may not be wearing a nurse's uniform, but I still get to nurture, guide, and make a difference — one lesson at a time.

Now I embrace this journey wholeheartedly — being a teacher isn't just what I'm working toward, it's what I'm meant to do.
